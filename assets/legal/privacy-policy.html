Privacy Policy

LifeQuest ("we", "our", "the app") is a life-simulation game. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Last updated: June 2026

Information we collect

  • Account data — If you sign in with Google or as a guest, Firebase Authentication stores a user ID and optional display name or email.
  • Game saves — Character progress is stored on your device and, when signed in, synced to Firebase Firestore.
  • Purchase records — In-app purchase transaction IDs and product IDs are stored to prevent duplicate grants and restore purchases.
  • Analytics — We log gameplay events (e.g. age up, character created) via Firebase Analytics to improve the app.
  • Advertising — Google AdMob may collect advertising identifiers to show ads to free users. You can remove ads via in-app purchase.

How we use information

  • Provide and save your game progress
  • Process in-app purchases and entitlements
  • Measure app performance and usage
  • Show ads (unless you purchase ad removal or premium)

Third-party services

We use Google Firebase (Auth, Firestore, Analytics, Cloud Functions) and Google AdMob. Their policies apply to data they process:

  • Firebase Privacy
  • Google Privacy Policy

Data retention

Save data is kept while your account is active. Local saves remain on your device until you delete the app or clear app data.

Your choices

  • Play as a guest without linking Google (limited cloud sync).
  • Purchase "Remove ads" or Premium to stop interstitial ads.
  • Request account or save deletion by contacting us (see below).

Children

LifeQuest is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: privacy@lifequest.app
